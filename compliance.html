Regulatory Standards We Meet

Our services are designed to help your organization comply with federal and industry-specific data protection regulations.

NAID AAA Certification

The gold standard in document destruction. Our facility and processes are subject to unannounced audits verifying security protocols, employee background checks, and operational standards.

HIPAA Compliance

We meet all Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act requirements for the destruction of Protected Health Information (PHI). BAA agreements provided.

FACTA Compliance

Fair and Accurate Credit Transactions Act requires proper disposal of consumer information. Our cross-cut shredding exceeds FACTA requirements for rendering data unreadable.

GLBA Compliance

Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act mandates financial institutions protect customer data. We provide the documented destruction required for GLBA Safeguards Rule compliance.

SOX Compliance

Sarbanes-Oxley Act requires proper retention and destruction of financial records. We provide destruction schedules aligned with SOX retention requirements.

State Privacy Laws

We comply with all 50 states' data disposal and privacy laws, including CCPA, providing the documentation needed for state-level regulatory compliance.

Technical Specifications

DIN 66399 International Shredding Standards

DIN 66399 is the globally recognized German standard for data destruction security levels. VaultShred standard cross-cut exceeds typical office strip shredders by an order of magnitude.

Security Level Classification Max Particle Size Recommended Application Reconstruction Risk
Level P-1 General Data Strip width ≤ 12 mm Non-sensitive internal notes Simple manual assembly
Level P-2 Internal Data Strip width ≤ 6 mm Outdated internal memos Reconstructible with tools
Level P-3 Sensitive Data Particle ≤ 320 mm² Confidential correspondence Feasible with specialized labs
Level P-4 Particularly Sensitive Particle ≤ 160 mm² (Cross-Cut) HIPAA, GLBA, FACTA, HR & Financial Records Impossible with current tech
Level P-5 Secret Data Particle ≤ 30 mm² Strategic patents, trade secrets, R&D Physically impossible
Level P-6 / P-7 Top Secret / NSA Particle ≤ 5 mm² National defense, military intelligence Zero residual trace
