Hardware Destruction

Non-Paper & Digital Media Destruction

Deleting or formatting digital drives does not erase data. We provide physical industrial de-manufacturing and degaussing compliant with NIST SP 800-88 Rev 1.

Rotational Hard Drives (HDDs)

DIN 66399 H-5 Shredding

High-torque dual-shaft shear blades crush platter discs and spin motors into irregular metallic scrap, preventing data recovery.

Solid State Drives (SSDs & Flash)

DIN 66399 E-4 Micro-Milling

NAND flash memory dies are pulverized to 2mm particle size to prevent electron microscopical chip reconstruction.

Magnetic Backup Tapes (LTO / DLT)

Degaussing + Physical Shear

High-flux demagnetization field wipes magnetic domains permanently, followed by physical transverse cross-cutting.

Optical Media (CD / DVD / Blu-Ray)

DIN 66399 O-4 Destruction

High-speed granulation breaks polycarbonate discs into miniature fragments, making laser readout impossible.

Corporate Uniforms & ID Badges

Brand Security Protection

Prevents impostor access into sensitive facilities by shredding branded corporate uniforms, security credentials, and keycards.

Microfilm & Microfiche Records

DIN 66399 F-4 Destruction

Specialized fine-cut shredders destroy photographic film archives, historical schematics, and medical microfilms.
