Our Journey

A Decade of Trusted Growth

From a local single-truck operation to one of the country's most respected names in certified data destruction.

2012

The First Mobile Unit

Founded in Metro City with a single high-torque mobile shredding truck and an unwavering dedication to on-site witnessed destruction.

2015

NAID AAA Certification

Awarded our first NAID AAA accreditation with a 100% score on unannounced security audits, setting our benchmark for operations.

2018

Hard Drive & E-Media Division

Expanded capabilities to include physical degaussing and shredding of solid-state drives, tapes, and classified electronic hardware.

2021

Zero-Landfill Milestone

Reached 100% post-destruction recycling efficiency, ensuring zero shredded fibers enter regional municipal landfills.

2024

Cloud Client Portal Launch

Introduced our proprietary real-time client dashboard with automated digital certificates, chain-of-custody tracking, and scheduling.

2026

5,000+ Enterprise Clients

Now safeguarding data for over 5,000 healthcare clinics, financial institutions, and corporate enterprises across the nation.
