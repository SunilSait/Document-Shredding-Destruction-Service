Office Strip Shredders vs. Certified VaultShred

Feeding paper into a personal office shredder leaves your business vulnerable to regulatory non-compliance, privacy breaches, and lost productivity.

In-House Office Shredding

High Liability & Operational Loss

Strip-Cut Vulnerability: Standard strip shredders produce ribbons that can be easily reconstructed using automated software.
Wasted Employee Labor: Staff spends 15-20 hours/month removing staples, unclogging feed jams, and emptying bins.
Zero Legal Audit Trail: No third-party certification or proof of disposal to present during federal compliance audits.
Internal Theft Exposure: Sensitive employee salaries, client credit cards, and patents are vulnerable while awaiting shredding.
Combustion & Health Risk: Fine paper dust buildup inside plastic office shredders poses documented fire and inhalation hazards.

VaultShred Certified Defense

Total Peace of Mind & Legal Immunity

DIN 66399 P-4 Cross-Cut: Particles reduced to tiny unreadable cross-cut confetti. Reconstruction is mathematically impossible.
Instant Productivity Reclaim: Employees simply drop documents into locked consoles — zero staple removal, zero labor required.
Official Certificate of Destruction: Legal notarized document protecting your firm in court and regulatory investigations.
Unbroken Chain of Custody: Locked tamper-proof containers and bonded, FBI-vetted security officers guarantee zero data leaks.
$5,000,000 Liability Warranty: Backed by comprehensive downstream environmental and legal indemnification policies.
